NightJar sees only what reaches Android. To see the blind spots for your phone, open Settings > About > **What
NightJar cannot see**.

## Blind spots

| Blind spot | Cause |
|---|---|
| Modem-only messages | Some networks send messages that the modem answers on its own without telling Android. NightJar can't see or block them. |
| RCS chats | Google Messages sends RCS chats over the internet. NightJar monitors SMS only. |
| After delivery | NightJar records when Android gives a message to an app, but not what the app does with it. |
| Before the first unlock | NightJar records these messages, but notifications can't show their content until you unlock the phone. |
| OnePlus and Oppo | Some builds pass messages through their own service first. NightJar then reports the message as handled by the phone maker. |

## Before the first unlock

After a reboot, the module uses its stored copy of your last rules right away, so holding and blocking work before you
unlock the phone. Some things wait for the unlock:

- Notifications show "Unlock the phone to see the details." until you unlock.
- Android keeps regular SMS until the unlock and processes them after it. NightJar checks them then.
- NightJar keeps up to 500 messages that arrive before the unlock in a staging store, and moves them to the history
  after the unlock.
- The self-test doesn't run before the first unlock.

## Limits of blocking

- **Silent SMS (Type 0).** Android discards a Type 0 message before NightJar can stop it. NightJar records it and can
  notify you, but can't hold or block it.
- **Messages for apps.** Visual voicemail, missed-call notices, carrier messages, chat setup, app-only codes and
  messages handled by the phone maker go to their app first. NightJar can only record them and notify you.
- **No delivery failure.** The network gets its confirmation before NightJar blocks a message, so the sender sees
  "delivered".
- **Unknown builds.** On a build NightJar doesn't know, a blocking hook may not attach. NightJar then only records
  messages.
- **CDMA.** NightJar doesn't support CDMA.

## After a system update

A system update can change the phone service. After an update, NightJar shows "Check again after the update". Run a
[self-test](/NightJar-Postmark/guide/self-test/). If a hook doesn't attach on the new build, Protection status shows that check
as not OK.

## The self-test

The self-test checks detection inside Android. It can't test messages that the modem handles on its own.
