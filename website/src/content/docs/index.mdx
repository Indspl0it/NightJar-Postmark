## Messages you don't see

Some SMS never reach your inbox. The network, your carrier, a SIM app or an attacker can send them, and NightJar
records each one.

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Silent SMS" icon="phone">
		Type 0 messages show nothing on screen. A sender can use them to check that a phone is on and to find where it
		is.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Settings pushes" icon="setting">
		WAP push messages that change the APN or proxy of your phone. An unexpected one can route your data through
		another server.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Location and SIM" icon="warning">
		SUPL location requests and SIM data download messages. The Simjacker and WIBattack attacks used the SIM path.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Malformed messages" icon="error">
		SMS and WAP push messages with a broken header, which attackers use to crash the phone service, as in
		CVE-2026-58882. The Recommended preset blocks them.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>

## How NightJar protects you

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="SMS firewall in the phone service" icon="padlock">
		NightJar checks each message inside the phone service, before Android passes it to an app or the SIM card.
		Choose Allow, Hold or Block for each type, or pick a preset. Your rules apply from boot, before you first unlock
		the phone. See [Protection](/NightJar-Postmark/guide/protection/).
	</Card>
	<Card title="Quarantine" icon="warning">
		A held message stays in NightJar's encrypted database, and no app receives it. Read what it is first, then
		release it to your messaging app or block it. See [held messages](/NightJar-Postmark/guide/held-messages/).
	</Card>
	<Card title="Attack detection" icon="magnifier">
		Detectors look for known attack patterns: a burst of silent SMS, a settings push without a network PIN, a command
		to the SIM browser (Simjacker, WIBattack) and a SIM that sends a message right after an update. See
		[detection](/NightJar-Postmark/features/detection/).
	</Card>
	<Card title="Evidence for each message" icon="document">
		Each message keeps its PDU, the decoded fields, the format errors and every step it took through the phone
		service. Export them as JSON, CSV or PDU hex for your own analysis.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Outgoing control" icon="phone">
		See the messages that apps and the SIM send from your phone, and stop an app from sending SMS. See
		[outgoing messages](/NightJar-Postmark/features/outgoing/).
	</Card>
	<Card title="Fails open, stays offline" icon="information">
		If NightJar fails, Android handles the message as if NightJar weren't installed. The app has no internet
		permission and keeps its data in an encrypted database. See [privacy](/NightJar-Postmark/safety/privacy/).
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
